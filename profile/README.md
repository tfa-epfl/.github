## About TFA

The Finance Association (TFA) is the finance association of EPFL.

Our mission is to:

- Bring together EPFL students interested in finance  
- Raise awareness of finance across campus  
- Build bridges between the student body and the financial sector  

TFA operates at the intersection of quantitative research, industry exposure, and applied financial practice.

---

## Mission

TFA aims to create a structured ecosystem where students can:

- Gain direct exposure to financial markets  
- Develop applied quantitative finance skills  
- Produce structured research deliverables  
- Connect with financial institutions
